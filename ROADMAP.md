Roadmap
Where Weeve is today and where it is headed. Open work is tracked in GitHub Issues; this file is the high-level view.
Shipped
Collaborative stories, invite-only or open, with text, audio, drawing and video contributions
Turn timers, story forking, and group and community spaces
Direct and group messaging, in-app and push notifications, follows and friends
Interest-based onboarding and a ranked discovery feed
Merit-based verification badges (Bronze, Silver, Gold)
Monetisation: token economy, creator earnings and payouts, and brand accounts
Referral links with native-app deep linking
Web, iOS and Android from one codebase (Next.js and Capacitor)
Admin console for users, stories, groups, communities and reports
